      About

      A medieval village that lives on its own, in real time. The villagers gather, farm, explore and build what they need. Paths appear where they walk. The Mayor only helps: picks crops, orders buildings, calls a rally.

      • Drag to move. Pinch, wheel or + / − to zoom.
      • Tap anything to see what it is.
      • Day, night and seasons follow the real clock in Bratislava.
